About the Problem Statement

SIH26007 · Mine Vehicle Safety in Fog

Open-cast iron-ore mines run Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM) dumpers around the clock — but during the June–October monsoon, dense fog cuts driver visibility to just 3–5 metres. Operators rely on manual monitoring with no real-time situational awareness, no shared hazard data between vehicles, and no central visibility of the entire mine — and in most remote pits, there's no internet to fall back on.

Team Vision-X is building HaulNex: an edge-AI safety co-pilot fitted to every dumper, designed to work exactly where connectivity and visibility both fail.

⚙ How HaulNex Works

End-to-end fog-safe perception, edge AI, and offline hazard sharing

From raw sensor fusion to a spoken warning in the driver's cab — every step runs onboard, with no cloud round-trip.

01
📡

Fog-Adaptive Sensing

Radar (TI IWR6843AOP) + thermal + RGB cameras fused onboard, seeing through fog RGB alone can't.

02
🧠

Edge-AI Risk Prediction

Jetson Orin Nano runs YOLOv8 + DeepSORT to track hazards and compute Time-to-Collision in real time.

03
🔊

Voice + LoRa Alerts

Driver gets instant voice guidance; hazards broadcast to nearby dumpers via LoRa V2V — no towers needed.

04
📊

Offline Digital Twin

LoRa V2I feeds a local mine-wide dashboard (FastAPI + React) for control-room visibility, fully offline.

Why RGB alone fails

Dense fog blinds the driver

  • Sightline collapse — fog scatters visible light, cutting sight distance to 3–5m.
  • Late reaction time — a dumper at 20km/h needs ~15m to stop; fog gives less than 5m of warning.
  • Invisible workers — pedestrians and rockfall blend into the haze until it's too late.
What thermal fusion reveals

HaulNex sees through the haze

  • Heat signatures — thermal imaging isn't scattered by fog droplets like visible light is.
  • Radar-confirmed range — mmWave radar adds hard distance data, day or night.
  • Early TTC warning — driver and nearby dumpers get a voice + LoRa alert seconds before RGB would even notice.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers grounded in the SIH26007 proposal

How does HaulNex work with no internet in the mine?

All hazard sharing runs over a SX1262 LoRa V2V/V2I mesh to a local mine-wide server — there's no cloud dependency, so it keeps working in the most remote pits.

What happens below 5m visibility in dense fog?

Radar and thermal imaging aren't scattered by fog droplets the way visible light is, so the fusion pipeline keeps detecting obstacles even when the RGB feed is nearly useless.

How fast does HaulNex warn the driver?

Time-to-Collision is computed onboard the Jetson Orin Nano in real time, triggering voice alerts instantly — no round-trip to any server is needed.

Can HaulNex be retrofitted onto existing dumpers?

Yes — it's designed as a bolt-on kit built around the Jetson Orin Nano compute unit in a rugged, IP-rated enclosure.

Is this validated on real mine data yet?

HaulNex is at prototype stage; field trials on fog-prone haul roads during the monsoon season are the next milestone.
